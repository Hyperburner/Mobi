Quick Reference: Points & Bidding Over Partner's 1♥

1. Card Symbols & Notation
Suits are always listed in order: ♠ Spades, ♥ Hearts, ♦ Diamonds, ♣ Clubs. A, K, Q, J are honour cards; each * is a small spot card (2–10) worth 0 high-card points.
2. High Card Points (HCP)
Ace (A) = 4 pts · King (K) = 3 pts · Queen (Q) = 2 pts · Jack (J) = 1 pt · Small card (*) = 0 pts.
3. Heart Fit & Distribution (TCP)
Partner's 1♥ promises 5+ hearts. With 3+ hearts in your hand, you have an 8+ card heart fit! Add short-suit points to get Total Card Points (TCP): Void (0 cards) = +5 · Singleton (1 card) = +3 · Doubleton (2 cards) = +1.
4. Your Response Bid Over 1♥
Pass = under 6 points · 2♥ = 3+ ♥ and 6–9 TCP · 3♥ = 3+ ♥ and 10–12 TCP · 4♥ = 3+ ♥ and 13–17 TCP.

Everything You Need for This Exercise

1. Reading Each Hand (♠ ♥ ♦ ♣)

Every bridge hand has 13 cards divided across the four suits: ♠ Spades, ♥ Hearts, ♦ Diamonds, and ♣ Clubs. Letters (A, K, Q, J) show your high cards, and each asterisk (*) stands for a small card (2 through 10).

2. High Card Points (HCP)

Start by adding up your honour cards:
Ace (A) = 4 · King (K) = 3 · Queen (Q) = 2 · Jack (J) = 1.
Small cards (*) count as 0 points.

3. Heart Fit & Total Card Points (TCP)

When partner opens 1♥, they promise at least 5 hearts. If you hold 3 or more hearts, your partnership has at least 8 hearts together—a fit! When you have a fit, add short-suit distribution points to your HCP:
Void (0 cards) = +5 · Singleton (1 card) = +3 · Doubleton (2 cards) = +1.

4. Choosing Your Response to 1♥

Once you have your Total Card Points (TCP) and 3+ hearts:
• Pass = fewer than 6 points
• 2♥ = 3+ hearts and 6–9 TCP
• 3♥ = 3+ hearts and 10–12 TCP
• 4♥ = 3+ hearts and 13–17 TCP
